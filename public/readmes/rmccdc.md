# RMCCDC and CCDC Invitationals

The Rocky Mountain Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition (RMCCDC) is the regional round of CCDC. Teams inherit a poorly secured corporate network, harden it while keeping business services up, and respond to injects from simulated management while a professional red team attacks. The regional champion advances to [National CCDC](/experience/nccdc).

## Results in brief

- **Qualifiers:** 1st place in every season from 2023-2024 through 2025-2026.
- **Regionals:** 1st place in 2023-2024 (as an alternate) and 2024-2025, both times advancing to Nationals; 2nd place in 2025-2026.
- **Invitationals:** between regional events the team trains at invitationals hosted by the Western (WRCCDC) and Midwest (MWCCDC) regions. The best of those runs was 1st place at MWCCDC Invitational #3 in 2025-2026.

## What teams have to do

- Secure Windows and Linux servers, Active Directory, DNS, web applications, and databases
- Keep services up and answer business injects on deadline
- Detect, contain, and recover from active intrusions
- Document incidents and communicate findings under time pressure

## Skills it exercises

- Active Directory hardening and monitoring
- Linux server security and service configuration
- Firewall rule management and network segmentation
- Incident response, log analysis, and forensic triage
- Patch management under adversarial conditions
- Team coordination when everything is on fire

## Why it matters

Competitions strip away the theoretical and expose what you actually know. There is no playbook that survives first contact and no second chance once a service is down. RMCCDC built the defensive instincts I lean on everywhere else.
