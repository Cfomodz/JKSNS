# Sandia National Laboratories

Competitions run inside Sandia National Laboratories, spanning machine learning and digital forensics.

## Events

- **Lab-Wide ML/DL Hackathon** (2026-2027): a laboratory-wide machine-learning and deep-learning hackathon. 1st place.
- **Internal ML Competition** (2025-2026): an internal machine-learning competition. 1st place.
- **TracerFire DFIR CTF** (2025-2026): Sandia's digital forensics and incident response exercise, played as a CTF in which teams reconstruct an intrusion from host and network evidence. 2nd place.
