# Other Cyber Defense Competitions

Defensive competitions outside the CCDC circuit.

- **Hivestorm** (2023-2024): a collegiate competition in which teams secure vulnerable Windows and Linux systems against a scoring engine within a fixed window.
- **eCitadel** (2024-2025): a CCDC-style defensive competition.
